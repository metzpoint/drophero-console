DropHero — Terms

For people doing DM work through DropHero.

What this is

DropHero is a platform for direct-message outreach work. You take assigned creators from the portal, send the message we give you from your own TikTok account, and report what comes back.

Your account details must be accurate

Use a real email address that you control, and real information about the TikTok accounts you use. Signing up on someone else's behalf, or with details that are not yours, is not allowed.

TikTok accounts need approval

A TikTok account you add is not usable until it has been approved. Approval can be withdrawn, and an account can be paused, at any time.

Work rules & performance requirements

Your work must aim to generate genuine replies and affiliate signups. Keep track of replies and follow up promptly using the guidance in Replies. No number of signups or amount of earnings is guaranteed.

100 DM / zero-reply rule: after you accept these terms at registration, the portal counts registered, non-void first-contact DMs to distinct creators across all TikTok accounts linked to your participant account. If at least 100 of those DMs have been recorded for 24 hours or longer and none has produced a verified reply, your outreach access is automatically blocked.

A reply screenshot that you have uploaded and that is awaiting review postpones this decision. Rejected or unsupported reply claims do not qualify. A negative reply still counts as a reply. This rule is a participation requirement; a zero-reply result alone is not proof of fraud.

A block stops new outreach work. Your recorded earnings and history are retained, and you can still upload reply evidence. Contact the DropHero team through the portal help to request a review. Reinstatement requires an owner review.

Send the DM before confirming it

Copying a message or opening TikTok does not send it. Confirm a send only after you have actually sent the assigned message to the correct creator. Report messages that could not be sent honestly. Multiple approved TikTok accounts are allowed and share one performance check. Do not use bots, fabricated identities, duplicate send claims or a new registration to bypass a block.

Upload genuine, current evidence

When requested, upload a clear screenshot showing the correct recipient and the relevant full conversation, including your sent message and the latest reply. Do not edit, recycle or misrepresent screenshots. Report new replies promptly and do not upload an old conversation as new work.

Follow through on replies

A red dot marks replies that need a response or review. Reminders may appear while the portal is open. Send suggested replies yourself in TikTok; copying them does not complete the work. Use the tracked affiliate signup link provided in the portal when the creator is ready. Do not promise fixed payments to creators: the affiliate offer is commission based.

Activity may be reviewed

Sends, screenshots, replies and earnings may be reviewed — by a person, automatically, or both — to check that the work was really done and to detect fraud or abuse.

What you are paid for

Payment is based on approved activity. Activity that has not been approved yet has not been earned yet. The current rates are shown in the portal.

Invalid activity is not paid

Activity that is fake, duplicated, falsely reported, or not supported by the evidence you submitted may be rejected, and rejected activity is not paid.

Access can be paused or ended

Your access, or a single TikTok account, may be paused or ended if these terms are broken or if the work is being misused.

You follow TikTok's rules

You are responsible for following TikTok's terms and any other platform rules that apply to the accounts you use. DropHero does not control those platforms and cannot make exceptions to their rules on your behalf.

These terms can change

DropHero may update these terms. The version below is the one you accepted; a newer version will carry a newer date. The performance rule above applies to new registrations that explicitly accept version 2026-10-05; it is not applied retroactively to existing agreements.
