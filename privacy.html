DropHero — Privacy

What we hold about you, and who else sees it.

What we hold

  • Account details — your email address and your sign-in credentials.
  • TikTok account information — the username, profile link and status of each account you use for this work, and the date you created it.
  • Work records — which creators were assigned to you, what was sent, when, and from which account.
  • Earnings and payment records — what each piece of approved work was worth, and what has been paid.
  • Proof screenshots and reply text — where the work requires evidence, the screenshots you upload and the reply text recorded with them. These may show a creator's messages as well as your own.

What we use it for

Only to run the service: to give you work, to keep accounts within their limits, to check that submitted work is genuine, to keep the service secure, and to pay you.

Who else processes it

  • Supabase — hosting, sign-in and the database. Everything above is stored there.
  • OpenAI — where a proof screenshot or reply text is checked automatically, its contents are sent to OpenAI for that check.
  • FirstPromoter — when you set up a payout, your email address and account name are sent there so a payout account can be created for you.

These are the parties involved in the DM work itself. This notice describes that work and does not claim to cover every law that may apply to you.

Contacting us

If you want to know what we hold about you, have something corrected, or have a question about any of the above, contact DropHero and we will answer.
