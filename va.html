Your DropHero guide

DropHero provides the creators, messages and next steps. You send the messages in TikTok and keep your conversations up to date here.

  1. Set up once. Create your DropHero account, confirm your email and add your TikTok profile using our bio, picture and video. The DropHero team reviews it before you can send.
  2. Send a DM. In Work, tap COPY DM & OPEN TIKTOK. Paste and send the message in TikTok. Return here and tap I SENT THE DM only after it really went through.
  3. Follow up. When a creator answers, find them in Chats and upload a screenshot of the latest messages. Replies prepares the next answer automatically. Copy it, send it in TikTok and upload their next reply.
My account is waiting for approval or on hold

Open Accounts to see its status. Waiting for approval means the DropHero team still needs to check the profile; you do not need to add it again. If TikTok shows a warning, restriction or login problem, use Report a problem on that account. A paused account needs the team's review before sending resumes.

When do I upload a screenshot?

Upload the creator's reply from Chats, or use Upload latest reply in Replies. Include their name and the latest messages. You do not need a screenshot for every first DM. If Work asks for a random Quick check, upload that sent message there and follow the status shown.

My suggested reply is still being prepared

Uploading starts the process automatically; you do not need to ask someone to activate it. Check Replies for Your turn to reply, Reading or checking the screenshot, and Waiting or finished. You can work on other creators while a check runs. If the portal asks for a clearer screenshot, upload one showing the latest conversation.

The message did not copy or TikTok did not open

Use the copy field shown in the portal if your phone blocks copying. If TikTok did not open, use Open TikTok profile on the same creator's card. Paste and tap Send yourself. If TikTok refuses the message, report the failed attempt instead of confirming it as sent.

I see a countdown, daily limit or no work

A countdown keeps your messages spaced out. Wait until it ends. Accounts shows each account's daily limit; Work shows how many messages you can still send. At the daily limit, return when the portal says you can send again. If there are no new creators, check Replies for conversations you can follow up.

How do the bonus and payouts work?

Use the creator's tracked signup link when it is available in their conversation. Earnings shows recorded amounts, verification and payout status. Uploading or copying alone is not a confirmed signup. Complete payout setup through the button in Earnings; payment details are entered securely with FirstPromoter.

I cannot sign in or I have not finished setup

Use your DropHero email address and password. Forgot it? Use Forgot your password? on the sign-in screen and open the latest recovery email. For an unfinished account, use Continue setup on the signup page. If invited, open the invitation link sent by the DropHero team.

What needs help from the DropHero team?

Profile approvals, paused accounts, incorrect creator links and payout account conflicts need the team's review. Report TikTok account problems through Accounts. For other unresolved issues, include the creator or account name and the exact message shown when contacting the DropHero team through your existing company contact.
